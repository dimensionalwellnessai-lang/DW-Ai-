Schedule builder, habit tracking, and routine management—all in one place.

📝 **Mood & Wellness Journaling**
Track your emotional landscape with mood logs, reflections, and pattern insights.

🎯 **Goal Management**
Set intentions, build habits, and create your own wellness system at your pace.

💡 **Personalized Insights**
Optional tools including moon cycle tracking, astrology charts, and pattern analysis for deeper self-reflection.

**[What Makes Us Different]**

✨ **Energy-Based, Not Productivity-Based**
We meet you where you are. Low energy? That's valid. We adjust.

💚 **Meaning Over Metrics**
No streaks. No guilt. No leaderboards. Just growth.

🌟 **Everything is Optional**
Use what serves you, skip what doesn't. Build your system your way.

🤝 **Consent-Based AI**
We always ask before saving, scheduling, or suggesting. Your wellness, your rules.

**[Social Proof / Trust]**
Trusted by users building sustainable wellness routines without the burnout.

**[Try Demo Mode]**
Not ready to sign up? Try our demo mode—explore the full app with pre-populated wellness data. No account needed.

**[Privacy]**
Your data stays yours. No selling, no spam, no BS. Read our privacy policy for details.

**[Call to Action]**
Download now and start building a wellness system that works for you.

## 🎨 App Store Visuals

### App Icon Guidelines
- **Focus**: Wellness/life planning (not astrology symbols)
- **Colors**: Calming (blues, greens, purples)
- **Symbol**: Consider a lightbulb, sunrise, or abstract wellness icon
- **Avoid**: Heavy astrological symbols (stars, zodiac signs) as primary element

### Preview Video (30-60 seconds)
**Recommended Flow**:
1. **Open on Demo Mode** (0-5s)
   - "Try it instantly with demo mode"
   
2. **Chat with AI Coach** (5-15s)
   - "Meet your wellness coach"
   - Show conversation
   
3. **Daily Planning** (15-25s)
   - "Plan your day with intention"
   - Show calendar/today view
   
4. **Habit Tracking** (25-35s)
   - "Build habits that stick"
   - Show goals and progress
   
5. **Optional Insights** (35-45s)
   - "Personalize your journey"
   - Quick glimpse of insights tab
   
6. **Call to Action** (45-50s)
   - "Start your wellness journey today"
   - Demo mode highlighted

**Music**: Calm, uplifting, energizing (not mystical/ethereal)
