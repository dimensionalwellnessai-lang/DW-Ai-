REIL’S LIFE SYSTEM

Weekly minute-by-minute schedule with the routine built into each block

MONDAY

Chest, shoulders, triceps day + app system review

6:00–6:05 AM — Wake up reset
	•	get out of bed immediately
	•	drink water
	•	do not open phone
	•	open blinds or turn on light

6:05–6:15 AM — Meditation routine
	•	sit upright
	•	inhale slowly through nose
	•	exhale slowly
	•	when your mind drifts, bring it back
	•	focus on calming your nervous system, not being “perfect”

6:15–6:35 AM — Morning activation routine
	•	20 bodyweight squats
	•	10 pushups
	•	plank 30 seconds
	•	rest briefly
	•	plank 30 seconds
	•	light shoulder rolls
	•	stand and stretch hips and hamstrings

6:35–7:00 AM — Hygiene routine
	•	shower
	•	brush teeth
	•	wash face
	•	deodorant
	•	get dressed for work
	•	check bag, keys, wallet, charger

7:00–7:20 AM — Breakfast routine
	•	cook 4 eggs with paprika, salt, pepper
	•	toast 2 slices of bread
	•	eat 1 banana
	•	drink water
	•	do not sit there scrolling while eating

7:20–8:20 AM — Commute routine
	•	leave on time
	•	use transit or bus plan first before defaulting to Uber
	•	while commuting: no doom scrolling
	•	either think through the day, listen to something useful, or just decompress

8:30 AM–12:00 PM — Work block
	•	focus on work tasks
	•	stay present
	•	do not mentally spiral into everything else you need to do later

12:00–12:30 PM — Lunch routine
	•	eat 7 oz chicken
	•	1 cup rice
	•	broccoli
	•	season with garlic powder, onion powder, soy sauce
	•	drink water
	•	sit down and actually finish the meal

12:30–5:00 PM — Work block
	•	finish shift
	•	keep notes if something life-related comes up that you need to remember later
	•	do not let work stress hijack the whole evening

5:00–5:30 PM — Transition home routine
	•	get home
	•	change clothes
	•	no laying down
	•	no disappearing into your phone
	•	mentally switch from work mode to home mode

5:30–5:45 PM — Clean reset routine
	•	wash dishes or load dishes
	•	take out trash if full
	•	wipe counters
	•	pick up anything left out
	•	put dirty clothes where they belong
	•	the goal is “reset the house,” not deep clean

6:00–6:45 PM — Workout routine: Chest, shoulders, triceps
Warm-up
	•	band pull-aparts x15
	•	pushups x10

Main workout
	•	band chest press 4 sets of 12
	•	pushups 4 sets of 10–15
	•	band shoulder press 3 sets of 10
	•	band lateral raises 3 sets of 12
	•	band tricep pushdowns 3 sets of 15

Finisher
	•	pushup hold for 20 seconds x2

Rest 45–75 seconds between sets
Move with control, not speed

7:00–7:30 PM — Dinner routine
	•	eat ground turkey with taco seasoning
	•	roasted or pan potatoes
	•	mixed vegetables
	•	sit and eat the meal, don’t graze

7:45–8:30 PM — App work routine: Monday system review
	•	open the app
	•	go page by page
	•	write down what feels off
	•	write down bugs
	•	write down confusing flows
	•	write down places that feel ugly, clunky, or not smooth
	•	do not fix anything yet
	•	this block is strictly for reviewing and collecting issues

8:30–8:40 PM — Snack routine
	•	Greek yogurt
	•	add honey
	•	water if needed

8:40–9:30 PM — Relax routine
	•	TV, movie, music, or quiet time
	•	no guilt
	•	this is earned rest
	•	try not to get pulled into distractions that knock off the whole night

9:30–10:00 PM — Wind-down routine
	•	lower lights
	•	stop active work
	•	bathroom
	•	set out what you need for tomorrow
	•	mentally close the day

10:00–10:05 PM — Hair wrap routine
	•	wrap hair
	•	quick hair check
	•	light spray if needed that night

10:30 PM — Sleep
