## Suggested usage limits
To keep app useful but encourage upgrades:
- **Free:** 7 core actions/day, 50/month  
- **Pro:** unlimited (or fair-use cap)

## Ads policy (important UX rule)
Free can have ads, but:
1. Never show ads during critical flows (onboarding, payment, recovery)
2. Keep ad frequency moderate (don’t overwhelm)
3. Show clear “Go Pro to remove ads”

Recommended:
- Banner ads on non-critical screens
- Optional rewarded ad for bonus free actions (optional)
