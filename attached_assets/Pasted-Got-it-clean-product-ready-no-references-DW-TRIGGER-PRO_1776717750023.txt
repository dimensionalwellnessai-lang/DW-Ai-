🧠 DW TRIGGER PROTOCOL

(Real-time Emotional Regulation System)

🎯 Purpose

Help users move from:
👉 reactive → aware → intentional

⸻

⚡ ENTRY POINT

User taps:
👉 “I feel triggered”

OR system detects:
	•	negative journal tone
	•	repeated behavior patterns
	•	high-stress inputs

⸻

🔁 STEP 1: IDENTIFY (10–20 sec)

Screen: “What’s happening right now?”

User selects:
	•	I feel anxious
	•	I feel disrespected
	•	I feel like I’m being lied to
	•	I feel rejected
	•	I’m overthinking

👉 Then:

“What are you assuming?”
	•	They’re cheating
	•	They don’t care about me
	•	I’m being taken advantage of
	•	Something is off

⸻

🧠 STEP 2: REALITY CHECK

Screen: “Pause. Let’s check this.”

👉 “Do you KNOW this is happening, or do you FEEL like it’s happening?”

Options:
	•	I have proof
	•	I don’t have proof

If “no proof”:
👉 “Your brain is trying to protect you, not confirm truth.”

⸻

⏸️ STEP 3: PAUSE TIMER (CORE)

Screen: “Don’t react yet.”

Options:
	•	5 min
	•	20 min
	•	30 min

During timer:
	•	breathing guide
	•	message: “You are safe. Let the reaction pass.”

⸻

🔍 STEP 4: ROOT CHECK

Screen: “Where is this coming from?”

Options:
	•	This reminds me of something from my past
	•	I’ve felt this before
	•	I’m afraid of being hurt / losing control

Optional:
👉 “What does this situation mean to you?”

⸻

🔄 STEP 5: REFRAME

Screen: “Choose a grounded perspective”

Options:
	•	“I don’t have enough information yet”
	•	“This feeling is real, but it may not be accurate”
	•	“I can respond calmly instead of reacting fast”

⸻

🗣️ STEP 6: RESPONSE BUILDER

Screen: “How do you want to respond?”

Recommended:
👉
“I’m feeling a little off right now and I’m working through it. Can we talk about it?”

or

“When this happens, I notice I feel insecure. I don’t want to react the wrong way, but I’d appreciate reassurance.”

⸻

🔒 STEP 7: PERSONAL STANDARD

User sees:
👉 “I don’t react without clarity”
or
👉 “I don’t accuse without evidence”

⸻

📊 STEP 8: TRACKING

“How did you handle this?”
	•	I reacted
	•	I paused first
	•	I communicated calmly

Track:
	•	trigger frequency
	•	reaction vs response
	•	improvement over time

⸻

🔁 WEEKLY INSIGHT

Example:
“You felt triggered 5 times this week.
4 of those had no confirmed issue.”

⸻

🔥 FLOW (SIMPLE)

Trigger → Identify → Reality Check → Pause → Reflect → Reframe → Respond → Track
