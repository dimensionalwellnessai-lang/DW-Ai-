## Quick Answers to Common Review Questions

**Q: Is this an astrology app?**
A: No. It's a wellness planner with AI coaching. Astrology is one optional personalization tool among many (moon cycles, patterns, insights).

**Q: Does the app require an account?**
A: No. Demo mode provides full functionality. Users can optionally create accounts to sync across devices.

**Q: Why do you need photo access?**
A: Optional feature for progress photos (body transformation tracking) and meal plan document uploads. App works fully without photos.

**Q: Is user data safe?**
A: Yes. Demo mode is local-only. Account data is encrypted. No selling user data. Clear privacy policy.

**Q: Who is this for?**
A: Anyone building wellness habits, managing daily routines, tracking mood, or seeking personalized guidance. Works for beginners to wellness enthusiasts.
