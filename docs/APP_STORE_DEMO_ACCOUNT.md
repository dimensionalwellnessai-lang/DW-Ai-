## App Store Submission Notes

### Unique Value Proposition

**Flip the Switch (Dimensional Wellness AI)** is distinct from typical wellness apps in several key ways:

1. **Energy-Based System** - Not prescriptive routines, but adaptive guidance based on user's current energy state
2. **13 Life Dimensions** - Holistic approach covering physical, emotional, spiritual, financial, intellectual, social, and more
3. **Consent-First Design** - Never mandatory, always asks before saving or scheduling
4. **Meaning Over Metrics** - No streaks or leaderboards that create pressure
5. **AI Concierge** - Proactive, pattern-aware assistant that learns user preferences

### Not Just Another Fitness/Wellness App

While the app includes fitness and wellness features common to the category, it differentiates through:

- **Nervous system awareness** - Adapts to user's capacity, not arbitrary goals
- **Life system integration** - All features work together in a unified approach
- **Silence as design** - Calm, unobtrusive UX without engagement manipulation
- **Dimensional approach** - Recognizes wellness as multifaceted, not single-focus

### Addressing Guideline 4.3(b) - Design Spam Concerns

The app includes optional "Energy Awareness" (previously labeled "Astrology") as a **dormant feature** that is:
- NOT enabled by default
- NOT prominently featured in the UI
- NOT the primary focus of the app
- Positioned as personal pattern awareness, not fortune-telling

The core app is a **comprehensive life management system** focused on:
- Goal tracking and achievement
- Habit formation
- Meal and workout planning
- AI-powered wellness coaching
- Schedule and routine management
- Multi-dimensional wellness tracking
